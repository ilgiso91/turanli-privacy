Privacy Policy - Florecho

Last Updated: September 25, 2026

This policy explains what Florecho: Plant Identifier (the "App"), an iOS app published by TuranliApp, does with your information. It covers Florecho only; where the general TuranliApp Privacy Policy differs, this page applies.

In short: no account, no ads, no tracking, and we do not sell your data. A photo leaves your device only when you start an AI scan, and a question only when you ask one. My Garden, care settings and reminders stay on your device. We keep no server copy of your scans.

3. How AI requests are protected

Requests are encrypted in transit. The App shrinks the photo before sending it, and the full OpenAI key is not stored in the App: AIProxy checks the device and forwards only authorized requests. Florecho asks OpenAI not to store the request, and OpenAI states that API data is not used to train its models unless the customer opts in (see OpenAI API data controls and the AIProxy Privacy Policy).

The anonymous identifiers are one-way hashes of a random value created for this installation. They are not based on your name, email, Apple Account, photos or advertising identifier, and deleting the App discards the random value.

4. What stays on your device

My Garden (plant photos, nicknames, notes, saved results and dates), care settings (pot, light, water need, last watering, reminder time) and App preferences are stored only on your device, protected by iOS Data Protection. They are never uploaded to us or to a provider, so we cannot restore them. Care reminders are scheduled by iOS on your device and show the plant's nickname, which may appear on your lock screen depending on your notification settings. Searching the built-in plant list sends nothing. You can delete a plant or a scan in the App; deleting the App removes everything, subject to your device backups.

5. What Florecho does not do

  • No account, and no request for your name, email, phone number, contacts, microphone, health data or location.
  • No advertising, no Advertising Identifier (IDFA), no tracking across other companies' apps or websites, and no Firebase or Google Analytics.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information for advertising.
  • No access to your payment-card details: purchases are made and paid through Apple. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy for how it handles purchase information.

6. Permissions

Camera, to photograph a plant, and for the light meter, which reads the camera's exposure settings without taking or sending a photo. Photos, to pick a photo through Apple's photo picker. Notifications, for your care reminders. All are optional and can be changed in iOS Settings.

AIProxy, OpenAI, Google, RevenueCat and Apple may process information in the United States and other countries. They do so under their own data-protection terms and transfer safeguards, such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask to access, correct, delete, restrict or port your data, object to processing, and withdraw consent. Because scans are not tied to an account and we keep no server copy, we usually cannot find a particular scan, but we will help with any request about data we control. Write to ismailturanli91@gmail.com; we normally reply within 30 days. You can also complain to a data-protection authority, such as the Austrian Datenschutzbehörde or the one where you live.

10. Children

Florecho is not directed at children under 13 and does not ask for a child's name, contact details or location. Children should use it with a parent or guardian and should not photograph people or type personal information. If you think a child has sent us personal information, contact us.
