3. PLANT PHOTOS AND AI ANALYSIS

3.1 When a photo leaves your device

Camera and photo-library access are optional. Florecho accesses only the photo you capture in the App or select through Apple's photo picker. After you choose to analyze it, the App resizes and compresses the image and sends it over an encrypted connection in this order:

StepProviderPurpose
1. Proxy and abuse protectionAIProxy (aiproxy.com)Verifies the App/device, enforces usage limits, and forwards the request without exposing the full AI-provider credential in the App
2. AI analysisOpenAI APIAnalyzes visible plant features and generates the structured result returned to Florecho

TuranliApp does not operate a separate server that stores the plant photo or AI result. The App also does not add the plant photo to its diagnostic event log.

Photograph plants only. Do not include people, faces, documents, addresses, screens, or other personal information in a scan. Anything visible in the selected image is transmitted to the processors above.

3.2 OpenAI processing

Florecho sends the request with storage disabled for the Responses API. OpenAI states that API inputs and outputs are not used to train its models by default unless the API customer explicitly opts in. OpenAI may nevertheless retain API content in abuse-monitoring logs for up to 30 days by default, or longer where legally required. For current details, see OpenAI API Data Controls.

3.3 AIProxy processing

According to AIProxy's published policy, it records the originating IP address, response status, request metadata such as input-token counts, and the response body only when a request returns an error status. AIProxy states that this information is retained for 30 days. See AIProxy Privacy Policy.

3.4 Anonymous safety identifier

Florecho creates a random identifier for the App installation, stores it locally, and sends only a one-way SHA-256 hash of that value to OpenAI as a safety identifier. It is used for abuse prevention and is not based on your name, email address, Apple Account, photo contents, or advertising identifier.

4. AI RESULTS AND SAFETY

AI output is automated and can be inaccurate, incomplete, or fabricated. Florecho presents confidence as an estimate and may ask for another photo instead of returning a definite identification. AI results are informational only and are not professional botanical, medical, veterinary, or emergency advice.

Never use an AI result to decide whether a plant, berry, mushroom, or other substance is edible or safe to touch, use, or give to a person or animal. For possible ingestion, poisoning, irritation, exposure, or symptoms, contact an appropriate emergency service, poison center, medical professional, or veterinarian promptly.

6. PURCHASES AND SUBSCRIPTIONS

Purchases are completed by Apple through the App Store. TuranliApp does not receive your payment-card number. Florecho uses RevenueCat to load subscription products, validate purchase receipts, determine whether Premium access is active, and restore purchases.

RevenueCat receives an anonymous App User ID and purchase history needed for these functions. Florecho does not attach a name, email address, or account profile to that anonymous identifier. RevenueCat also uses purchase data for service analytics and fraud prevention under its Privacy Policy.

7. INFORMATION STORED ON YOUR DEVICE

Florecho stores limited preferences locally, including whether onboarding has been completed, Sound Garden session state, and the random installation identifier described above. A photo selected for analysis is held for the active scan flow; Florecho does not upload it to a TuranliApp-owned photo library or account.

Deleting the App removes Florecho's locally stored preferences and installation identifier, subject to any device backup behavior controlled by Apple and your device settings. Deleting the App does not cancel an App Store subscription; subscriptions are managed in your Apple Account settings.

8. ANALYTICS, ADVERTISING, AND TRACKING

The current version of Florecho contains no advertising SDK and no cross-app or cross-site tracking SDK. It does not request App Tracking Transparency permission.

Florecho records limited fixed screen and feature events in Apple's local unified system log for debugging. These events do not contain plant photos or free-form user notes, and the current version does not transmit them to Firebase Analytics or another TuranliApp analytics backend. AIProxy and RevenueCat process the limited service metadata described in Sections 3 and 6.

No transmission or storage method is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
