Last Updated: October 5, 2026

This policy explains what Charm AI: Rizz App & Wingman (the "App"), an iOS app published by TuranliApp, does with your information. It covers Charm AI only; where the general TuranliApp Privacy Policy differs, this page applies.

In short: Charm AI is for adults aged 18 or older. There is no account, no advertising and no cross-app tracking, and we do not sell your data. Conversation text, screenshots, photos and imported chats leave your device only after you allow AI data sharing and start an AI action. A Chat Wrapped export is read on your device; only a limited message sample and statistics are sent for AI insights. History, favorites and settings stay on your device.

4. Chat Wrapped on your device

When you select a WhatsApp, Instagram or Messenger export, the App reads it on your device to find the conversation and calculate statistics. To read it, the App may make a temporary copy inside its own storage; it tries to delete that copy after reading and removes leftovers the next time you import, and iOS may also remove temporary files. The statistics and insights you see are not uploaded to us.

5. How AI requests are protected

Requests are encrypted in transit and pass through AIProxy, which applies security checks before forwarding them. OpenAI states that API data is not used to train its models unless the customer opts in (see OpenAI API data controls and the AIProxy Privacy Policy). Content is also checked by OpenAI's safety (moderation) service before a result is generated.

6. Free uses, Premium and fair use

A small free-use counter is kept in your device's secure storage and may remain on the device after the App is deleted. It contains no conversation content. Premium is not unlimited: AI requests from free and Premium users are subject to fair-use and security limits. If a limit is reached, the App tells you, and AI features become available again later.

7. What stays on your device

History, favorites, your writing-style summary (the raw samples are not saved), language and App preferences, and your subscription status are stored only on your device, in storage shared between the App and the Charm keyboard. They are not uploaded to us, so we cannot restore them. The reset option in Profile clears these records; deleting the App removes them, except the free-use counter described in Section 6 and your device backups. Notifications are optional and scheduled by iOS on your device.

8. What Charm AI does not do

  • No account, and no request for your name, email, phone number, contacts, microphone, health data or precise location.
  • No advertising inside the App, no Advertising Identifier (IDFA), and no tracking across other companies' apps or websites.
  • No selling or sharing of conversation content or personal information for advertising.
  • No access to your payment-card details: purchases are made and paid through Apple. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy for how it handles purchase information.

9. Permissions

Photos: screenshots and profile photos are chosen through Apple's photo picker, so the App receives only the images you select and does not ask for access to your photo library. Keyboard Full Access, so the Charm keyboard can send a reply request you start and read your subscription status. Notifications, for optional reminders. Full Access and Notifications are optional and can be changed in iOS Settings.
