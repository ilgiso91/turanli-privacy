Support - EmberHome

Last Updated: September 23, 2026

EmberHome is a shared home app for the people you live with: one chore list, one shopping list, and a clear picture of who does what. This page answers the most common questions. If yours is not here, write to us - see Contact.

1. HOW EMBERHOME WORKS

  • Home shows your rooms and how much attention each one needs.
  • Tasks is the shared chore list. Each task has a room, a person it is assigned to, a due date, and points.
  • Shop is the shared shopping list. Anyone in the home can add or tick off items.
  • Progress shows the points and streaks your home has earned.
  • Profile holds your household, Ember Plus, sign-out, and account deletion.

Everything you add is shared with the other members of your home and appears on their phones.

2. CREATING OR JOINING A HOME

Creating a home

After you sign in, choose Create a home and give it a name. You become its owner.

Inviting people

Owners and admins can share an invite code (it looks like EH-XXXX-XXXX) from Profile > The household. A code works for seven days and for up to twelve people. Creating a new code cancels the previous one.

Joining a home

Choose Join with code and enter the code you were given. New members join as roommates; the owner can make them admins later.

"That invite code has expired" or "is not active"

The code is older than seven days, has been replaced by a newer code, or has reached its member limit. Ask the owner or an admin for a fresh code.

"This account already belongs to another household"

An account can be a member of one home at a time.

3. ACCOUNT AND SIGN-IN

Which sign-in options are there?

Sign in with Apple, or an email address and password. Email accounts need to confirm their address before creating or joining a home.

I did not receive the verification email

Check your spam or junk folder, then use Send the verification email again in the App. Make sure the address you typed is correct.

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen, enter your email address and tap Forgot password? Send a reset email.

4. EMBER PLUS

EmberHome is free to use. Ember Plus is an optional subscription that adds:

  • Recurring tasks that reset themselves daily, weekly, or monthly.
  • Household insights on the Progress screen that show who might need a hand.

It is available as a monthly or yearly subscription. The yearly plan may begin with a free trial; the price and trial length are shown before you confirm. Payment is charged to your Apple Account and renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.

How do I cancel?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose EmberHome. You keep Ember Plus until the end of the period you have paid for.

I bought Ember Plus but do not have it

Make sure you are signed in to the same EmberHome account you used when you subscribed, then open Profile > Ember Plus and tap Restore purchases.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by Apple. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

5. YOUR DATA AND DELETING YOUR ACCOUNT

You can delete your account at any time in Profile > Delete account. You will be asked to confirm with your password or with Sign in with Apple.

  • If you are the last member of your home, the home and all of its tasks, shopping list, and invite codes are deleted too.
  • If other people still live in the home, it stays with them. Tasks assigned to you become unassigned, and if you owned the home, another member takes it over.

Deleting your account does not cancel Ember Plus. Cancel the subscription in your Apple Account settings (see Section 4) to stop future charges.

Details are in the EmberHome Privacy Policy.

6. OTHER COMMON QUESTIONS

My changes are not showing on my housemate's phone

Both phones need an internet connection. Changes made offline are sent as soon as the connection returns. If a change still does not appear, close and reopen the App on both phones.

Which languages does EmberHome support?

English, Turkish, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Dutch, and Brazilian Portuguese. The App follows your iPhone's language.
