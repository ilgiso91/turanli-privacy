1. HOW FLORECHO WORKS

You take or choose a photo and pick what you want to know: identify a plant, look at visible signs of stress, see its known hazards, or name a tree, weed, pest or bird. The photo is sent for AI analysis and comes back with the most likely identification, what the photo shows, and known hazards worth keeping in mind. You can save a plant to My Garden with a nickname, a cover photo, your own notes and care reminders.

Before your first AI request, Florecho asks you to accept the Terms of Use and the processing described in the Privacy Policy. Nothing is sent before that.

Florecho does not require an account. It contains no advertising and no cross-app tracking.

AI results are estimates and can be wrong. Florecho is not a substitute for professional botanical, medical or veterinary advice. Never use it to decide whether a plant, berry or mushroom is edible or safe to touch. If you suspect poisoning, contact a poison center, doctor or veterinarian.

Free requests

Each free request comes back 24 hours after you used it, so the app shows when the next one is available. A request is counted only when the AI answered; a scan that failed on the way is not counted. The count is kept on your iPhone.

Premium

Florecho Premium is an auto-renewing subscription, yearly with a 3-day free trial or monthly. The price in your currency is shown in the app before you buy. Premium is not unlimited: every user, subscribers included, has a daily limit. If you reach it, the app says:

"You have reached today's plant analysis limit. Please try again tomorrow."

The Premium allowance refills gradually, about one request every 48 minutes, rather than all at once at midnight.

Restore a purchase

On a new iPhone or after reinstalling, open Settings in Florecho and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account that made the purchase.

Cancel or get a refund

Apple handles billing, so subscriptions are managed in your Apple Account, not in Florecho: on your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends to avoid the next charge; you keep Premium until the end of the period you paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. For a refund, use reportaproblem.apple.com; we cannot issue refunds ourselves.

3. "NOT IDENTIFIED - ANOTHER PHOTO IS NEEDED"

This message does not mean the plant is rare or unknown to Florecho. It means the photo does not support naming the plant at all: no plant is visible, or the subject is too small, too blurred, too dark, or too hidden behind other things to name even a likely genus. When this happens, Florecho tells you which photo would help instead of guessing.

If Florecho can name a likely plant but a distinguishing detail is missing, it does not show this message. It gives you the most likely candidate at the confidence the photo supports, and tells you under "next step" which extra photo would confirm it. You can add a second photo of the same plant from another angle; both are analyzed together as one request.

Photos that work better

  • One plant filling most of the frame, rather than a whole flower bed.
  • Daylight or bright indoor light, with the leaf surface in focus.
  • A leaf photographed from above, showing its shape, edge and veins.
  • A flower or fruit in the frame when the plant has one - these are often what settles an identification.

4. CONFIDENCE, AND WHY IT IS NEVER "CERTAIN"

Every identification is a probability, never a confirmation. Florecho reports one of three levels, and may add up to two alternative candidates worth considering.

LevelWhat it means
HighSeveral distinctive visible traits, with no meaningful close alternative. Still "most likely", not certain.
MediumThe photo supports a likely match but at least one distinguishing trait is missing.
LowSeveral plants remain plausible, or the photo quality is not sufficient.

Florecho also lists the visible traits an identification is based on, so you can judge for yourself whether it looked at the right things. In a stress check, each observation is rated Low, Moderate or High for how much of it the photo shows. That is the visible extent, not a probability or a diagnosis.

5. KNOWN HAZARDS

Known Hazards reports what is known about a plant's hazards to people and pets - irritating sap or toxic leaves, for example - for the plant Florecho believes it is. It is free for everyone.

Florecho never tells you that a plant is safe. An empty hazard list means nothing was found for this identification, not that the plant is harmless, and a list is only as reliable as the name it was looked up for. Keep children and animals away from an unfamiliar plant until a qualified person has identified it. If you suspect poisoning, contact a poison center, doctor or veterinarian.

Florecho does not diagnose or treat people or animals. It is designed never to describe eating, cooking or medicinal use of a plant, and never to give medication or chemical dosages. Questions about a person's or an animal's health are declined.

6. MY GARDEN AND YOUR DATA

Plants you save to My Garden - nickname, cover photo, notes, care reminders and scan history - stay on your iPhone. They are not uploaded to us and we cannot see them.

  • Delete a single plant or scan inside My Garden.
  • Deleting the app removes My Garden and its local settings, subject to your device backups.
  • Because everything is stored on the device, My Garden does not currently sync between devices.

What leaves your device, and who processes it, is described in full in the Florecho Privacy Policy.

7. OTHER COMMON QUESTIONS

A scan will not start, or fails immediately

Scans need an internet connection. If the app reports that analysis is temporarily unavailable, try again in a few minutes. If it keeps failing, write to us with your iOS version.

Florecho asks for camera or photo access

The camera is used only for the photo you take and for the light meter, and photo access only for the photo you choose. You can change both at any time in iOS Settings. A photo leaves your device only when you start a scan; the light meter's readings never leave it.

Which languages does Florecho speak?

Florecho is available in 15 languages: English, Arabic, Dutch, French, German, Hungarian, Indonesian, Italian, Japanese, Polish, Portuguese, Spanish, Thai, Turkish and Vietnamese. Results come back in the app's language, which follows your iPhone's language settings.

Sound Garden

The soundscapes are generated on your device as you listen; nothing is downloaded or streamed. They are there to make a care routine feel calmer. We do not claim that they heal plants or improve their growth.

A result looks wrong

Please tell us. Send the photo if you are willing, along with what Florecho said and what the plant actually is. Reports like these are how the app's cautiousness gets tuned.
