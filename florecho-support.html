1. HOW FLORECHO WORKS

You take or choose a photo of a plant and start a scan. The photo is sent for AI analysis and comes back with the most likely identification, what the photo shows about the plant's condition, and known hazards worth keeping in mind. You can save a plant to My Garden with a nickname, a cover photo and your own notes.

Florecho does not require an account. It contains no advertising and no cross-app tracking.

AI results are estimates and can be wrong. Florecho is not a substitute for professional botanical, medical or veterinary advice. Never use it to decide whether a plant, berry or mushroom is edible or safe to touch. If you suspect poisoning, contact a poison center, doctor or veterinarian.

2. DAILY SCAN LIMIT

Florecho is free, and each device may run up to 10 AI scans per day. The limit protects the service from abuse and keeps the app free for everyone.

When you reach it, the app says:

"You have reached today's plant analysis limit. Please try again tomorrow."

The allowance is not restored all at once at midnight. It refills gradually over the following 24 hours, so a single scan becomes available again roughly every two and a half hours after you run out.

The limit applies per device and is counted without identifying you. Reinstalling the app to reset the count does not work, and it deletes everything you saved in My Garden.

3. "NOT IDENTIFIED - ANOTHER PHOTO IS NEEDED"

This message does not mean the plant is rare or unknown to Florecho. It means the photo does not support naming the plant at all: no plant is visible, or the subject is too small, too blurred, too dark, or too hidden behind other things to name even a likely genus. When this happens, Florecho tells you which photo would help instead of guessing.

If Florecho can name a likely plant but a distinguishing detail is missing, it does not show this message. It gives you the most likely candidate at the confidence the photo supports, and tells you under "next step" which extra photo would confirm it.

Photos that work better

  • One plant filling most of the frame, rather than a whole flower bed.
  • Daylight or bright indoor light, with the leaf surface in focus.
  • A leaf photographed from above, showing its shape, edge and veins.
  • A flower or fruit in the frame when the plant has one - these are often what settles an identification.

4. CONFIDENCE, AND WHY IT IS NEVER "CERTAIN"

Every identification is a probability, never a confirmation. Florecho reports one of three levels, and may add up to two alternative candidates worth considering.

LevelWhat it means
HighSeveral distinctive visible traits, with no meaningful close alternative. Still "most likely", not certain.
MediumThe photo supports a likely match but at least one distinguishing trait is missing.
LowSeveral plants remain plausible, or the photo quality is not sufficient.

Florecho also lists the visible traits an identification is based on, so you can judge for yourself whether it looked at the right things.

5. SAFETY CHECK

Safety Check reports known hazards to people and pets - irritating sap or toxic leaves, for example - when the photo supports naming them.

Florecho never tells you that a plant is safe. An empty hazard list means nothing was identified from this photo, not that the plant is harmless. Always confirm with a poison-control service or an ASPCA listing, and with a veterinarian for anything concerning an animal.

Florecho does not give medical or veterinary treatment instructions, and does not provide medication or chemical dosages.

6. MY GARDEN AND YOUR DATA

Plants you save to My Garden - nickname, cover photo, notes and scan history - stay on your iPhone. They are not uploaded to us and we cannot see them.

  • Delete a single plant or scan inside My Garden.
  • Deleting the app removes My Garden, its local preferences and the installation identifier.
  • Because everything is stored on the device, My Garden does not currently sync between devices, and deleting the app cannot be undone.

What leaves your device, and who processes it, is described in full in the Florecho Privacy Policy.

7. OTHER COMMON QUESTIONS

A scan will not start, or fails immediately

Scans need an internet connection. If the app reports that analysis is temporarily unavailable, try again in a few minutes. If it keeps failing, write to us with your iOS version.

Florecho asks for camera or photo access

The camera is used only for the photo you take, and photo access only for the photo you choose. You can change both at any time in iOS Settings. A photo leaves your device only when you start a scan.

The answers are in English

Florecho 1.0 is available in English, and results come back in English. We have not committed to a date for other languages.

Sound Garden

The soundscapes are generated on your device as you listen; nothing is downloaded or streamed. They are there to make a care routine feel calmer. We do not claim that they heal plants or improve their growth.

Does Florecho cost anything?

Version 1.0 is free and contains no in-app purchases and no subscription.

A result looks wrong

Please tell us. Send the photo if you are willing, along with what Florecho said and what the plant actually is. Reports like these are how the app's cautiousness gets tuned.
