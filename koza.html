Privacy Policy - Koza

Last Updated: August 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Koza (the "App"), a pregnancy tracking application for iOS published by TuranliApp, collects, uses, stores, and protects your information.

This policy covers Koza only. TuranliApp publishes other applications that have different features and different data practices; those are covered by our general Privacy Policy. Where the two differ, this document governs your use of Koza.

Koza is a health application. We have written this policy to describe precisely what the App does, rather than what pregnancy apps generally do. In particular, most of the health information you enter never leaves your device, the App contains no advertising and no tracking software, and none of your health information is ever sent to an analytics or crash reporting service. The App does measure how its screens are used, within the strict limits described in Section 7.

By downloading, installing, or using Koza, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

Because this information is held only on your device, it is removed when you delete the App, and it is included in your device backups if you have those enabled. It is not recoverable by us.

  • We do not collect your location. The App requests no location permission.
  • We do not collect advertising identifiers. The App does not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA) and does not include the Firebase component that would collect it.

  • We do not collect payment information. Where Koza offers optional subscriptions, the purchase is completed by Apple and your payment details are handled by Apple, never by us.

5. SHARING A PREGNANCY WITH YOUR PARTNER OR FAMILY

Koza lets you share a pregnancy with other people, such as your partner. This works through an invite code that you generate in the App and give to the other person.

Please read this carefully. When someone joins your pregnancy using your invite code, they become a member of that pregnancy. Members can read and write the shared information listed in Section 2.1 - including your weight entries, blood pressure values recorded with them, kick history, appointments, and letters.

Anyone who obtains your invite code can join. Share it only with people you intend to give this access to.

Access is enforced on our servers: only accounts listed as members of a pregnancy can read or write its data. Information stored only on your device (Section 2.2) is never shared with family members.

6. THE IN-APP ASSISTANT

Koza includes an assistant screen where you can ask general questions about pregnancy.

6.1 What is sent

Only the text you type into the assistant. Nothing else is transmitted.

Your records are never sent to the assistant, and the assistant cannot read them. We do not transmit your name, email address, account identifier, pregnancy or due dates, weight, blood pressure, blood glucose, symptoms, kick history, appointments, letters, journal entries, mood, water intake, step count, or photos. Your question travels on its own, with nothing attached that identifies you or your pregnancy.

6.2 How it is processed

Your message travels over an encrypted connection to two providers, in this order:

StepProviderWhat it does
1. ProxyAIProxy (aiproxy.com)Receives your message and forwards it to the AI provider. It exists so that our API credentials never have to be stored inside the App, where they could be extracted.
2. AI providerOpenAI, L.L.C.Generates the reply, which is returned to your device through the same path.

Both providers are located in the United States, so your message is processed there - see Section 12.

We ourselves do not store your messages or the replies, and we do not associate them with your account. We operate no server of our own in this path.

What AIProxy records. According to its published privacy policy, AIProxy logs the originating IP address of the request, the HTTP status code of the response, metadata about the request such as the number of input tokens, and - only when a request fails with an error - the body of the error response. Your IP address is therefore visible to AIProxy even though nothing in the message identifies you. AIProxy does not publish a retention period for these logs.

What OpenAI does with it. OpenAI processes content submitted through its API as a service provider acting on our behalf. Under its API terms, this content is not used to train its models, and it is retained for a limited period for abuse monitoring before being deleted.

6.3 Please keep in mind

  • Do not type personal or identifying details into the assistant. Because only what you type is sent, what you choose to write is what determines how much leaves your device. Avoid your name, contact details, or anything you would not want to send to a third party.
  • Your IP address reaches the proxy. As with any internet request, the connection itself carries your IP address, and AIProxy logs it. We do not receive it and cannot link it to your account.
  • Answers are generated by artificial intelligence. They are not written by us and are not reviewed by a physician, midwife, or other healthcare professional before you see them. They may be inaccurate, incomplete, or fabricated. The assistant does not provide medical advice; Section 15 of this policy and Section 4.1 of the Terms of Use apply to it in full.

Two cases where nothing leaves your device. First, if what you write matches a list of urgent pregnancy symptoms built into the App, the App answers immediately from that built-in list and no request is sent at all; it tells you to contact your doctor or emergency services without consulting any server. Second, if the connection or the AI provider fails, the App falls back to a fixed set of answers stored inside the App itself.

7. ANALYTICS, ADVERTISING, AND TRACKING

Koza contains no advertising software and no cross-app or cross-site tracking software. It does contain analytics and crash reporting, provided by Google LLC (Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics), used only to see which screens are used and to find crashes.

Your health information is never sent to these services. No event ever carries your name, email address, pregnancy or due dates, pregnancy week, weight, blood pressure, blood glucose, symptoms, kick history, appointments, letters, journal entries, mood, photos, or anything you type - including anything you type into the assistant.

What is measured:

  • Which screen you are on, by a fixed internal screen name such as paywall or onboarding_role.
  • Progress through the sign-up flow: which step, its number, whether you chose the mother or partner role, and whether the step was completed.
  • The outcome of a sign-in or sign-up attempt, and which method was used (email, Apple, or Google).
  • Subscription screen interactions: that the screen was opened, from where, which plan was highlighted, and whether a purchase or restore was started and how it ended. No payment details are involved.
  • Whether the review prompt was shown and what you did with it.
  • Crash and error reports, with the screen name and a short internal label describing where the error occurred.

How you are identified in this data: not by your account identifier. Before any identifier is sent, it is replaced with a one-way SHA-256 hash, which cannot be reversed to recover the original. Your role (mother or partner) and whether subscriptions are enabled in your build are recorded as properties. Firebase additionally assigns its own app-instance identifier, which is reset if you delete and reinstall the App.

  • The App does not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA). It links the Firebase analytics component that excludes advertising-identifier collection.
  • The App does not use the App Tracking Transparency framework and will never ask for permission to track you, because it does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We do not build advertising profiles, and we do not sell or share your data with data brokers.
  • The App does not report screen names automatically; every event listed above is sent explicitly by code we wrote, which is why we can state the list exhaustively.

10. DATA RETENTION AND DELETION

We retain the information in Section 2.1 for as long as your account exists.

Assistant messages. We keep no copy of them. Their retention is determined by the two providers in Section 6.2: OpenAI retains API content for a limited period for abuse monitoring before deleting it, and AIProxy retains the request logs described there without publishing a retention period. Because we hold no copy and nothing in a message is linked to your account, we cannot retrieve or delete an individual message on request.

Analytics and crash reports. These are retained by Google LLC according to the retention setting of our Firebase project. Deleting your account does not delete them, because they are not linked to your account identifier - only to a one-way hash of it, as described in Section 7.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account from within the App, under Profile > Delete My Account. This is a permanent deletion, not a sign-out. It removes:

  • Your Firebase Authentication account
  • Your user record
  • Every pregnancy you own, together with its weight entries, kick history, appointments, and letters
  • Every family invite code associated with those pregnancies

Deleted data cannot be recovered. Backup copies held by our infrastructure provider are overwritten in the ordinary course of their retention cycle.

Deleting device-only data

Information listed in Section 2.2 is removed when you delete the App from your device.

Apple Health data

Data that Koza has written to Apple Health remains in Apple Health after you delete the App. You control it in the iOS Health app.

11. SECURITY

  • All communication between the App and our infrastructure is encrypted in transit using TLS.
  • Data stored in Cloud Firestore is encrypted at rest by the infrastructure provider.
  • Server-side security rules restrict every record to the account that owns it, or to the members of the relevant pregnancy. A user cannot read another user's data by any means available in the App.
  • Health information kept on your device is protected by your device passcode and iOS data protection.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
