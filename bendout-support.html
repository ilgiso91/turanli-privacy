1. How to play

Tap an arrow and it slides out along its own path, but only if the way from its head to the edge of the board is clear. Clear every arrow to win the level. If another arrow is in the way, the tapped arrow shakes and stays.

Every level starts with three stars. The first blocked tap on a level is only a warning, and big boards forgive one more for every 20 arrows. After that, each blocked tap costs a star. Losing all three stars loses the level and costs one life. Pinch to zoom on big boards; a tap between two arrows does nothing, so a near miss never moves the wrong one.

2. Lives, gold and boosters

You have up to five lives. One life comes back every 30 minutes, or you can refill them all with gold. You earn gold by winning levels.

  • Hint shows an arrow that can go now.
  • Shield takes the next mistake on the level instead of a star.
  • Smash removes any arrow, blocked or not.

Boosters are bought with gold in the Shop. Gold, lives and boosters have no money value and cannot be exchanged or transferred; see the Terms of Use.

3. Your progress on a new iPhone

Bendout has no account. Your levels, stars, gold, lives and boosters are saved on your iPhone and, if iCloud is on, backed up to your own iCloud account. On a new iPhone, or after reinstalling, sign in with the same Apple Account with iCloud turned on and open Bendout: your progress comes back, sometimes a minute or two after the first launch. A level you left half done resumes where you stopped.

Without iCloud we cannot restore progress, because we never receive it.

4. Purchases

Restore "No ads". Open Settings in Bendout and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the Apple Account that made the purchase. This brings back "No ads", including the one in the starter pack. Gold, lives and boosters you bought come back through your iCloud backup, not through Restore.

A purchase did not arrive. Close and reopen Bendout with an internet connection; a confirmed purchase is delivered on the next launch, once. If it is still missing, email us your Apple receipt.

Refunds. Apple handles payments, so refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com; we cannot issue refunds ourselves.

5. Ads

Bendout is free and shows ads, kept rare on purpose: none before level 10, only after a win and never after a loss, at most after every third win and at least three minutes apart. Videos only play when you choose a reward. The one-time "No ads" purchase removes the ads between levels; reward videos you choose stay available.

Ads are limited to content suitable for teens, and categories such as gambling, dating, alcohol and sexual content are blocked. If you still see an ad that seems wrong, write to us with the advertiser's name and we will block it.

Ad privacy choices. In the EU, the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and U.S. states with privacy laws, Bendout shows Google's privacy form before ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings › Privacy › Ad privacy choices.

6. Language

Bendout follows your iPhone's language and is available in English and 26 other languages. To play in another language, open the iPhone Settings app, tap Apps › Bendout › Language.
