Privacy Policy - Bendout

Last Updated: November 2, 2026

This policy explains what Bendout (the "App"), an arrow puzzle game for iPhone published by TuranliApp, does with your information. It covers Bendout only; where the general TuranliApp Privacy Policy differs, this page applies.

In short: no account, no request to track you, no analytics service of our own, and we do not sell your data. Your progress stays on your device and in your own iCloud account. Bendout is free and shows ads from Google AdMob; in the EU, the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google first asks for your consent. Purchases are paid through Apple and confirmed by RevenueCat.

3. What stays on your device

Your progress, gold, lives and boosters, levels in progress, your vibration setting, a record of purchases already delivered, the counters the App uses to space out ads, offers and rating requests, Google's record of your consent choices, and RevenueCat's App User ID and purchase status are stored on your device. Of these, only levels cleared, stars, best times, gold, lives and boosters are also copied to your iCloud backup (section 2). A protected copy of your gold, lives and boosters is also kept in the device's Keychain, which never leaves the device but which iOS may keep after the App is deleted. Deleting the App removes everything else on the device, subject to your device backups.

4. What Bendout does not do

  • No account, and no request for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, location or health data.
  • No App Tracking Transparency prompt and no Advertising Identifier (IDFA).
  • No analytics or crash-reporting service of our own. If you allow it in iOS Settings, Apple shares anonymous crash reports with us.
  • No selling of your personal information for money.
  • No access to your payment-card details: purchases are made and paid through Apple.

5. Legal bases (GDPR and similar laws)

  • Contract: providing the game, the iCloud backup and the purchases you make, including restoring them.
  • Consent: storing and reading information on your device for advertising, and personalised ads, in the EU, the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, through Google's consent form. You can withdraw consent at any time under Settings › Privacy › Ad privacy choices; this does not affect processing before you withdrew it.
  • Legitimate interests: funding the free game with limited ads, and showing, counting and capping those ads, preventing ad and purchase fraud, and answering your emails.
  • Legal obligation: where the law requires it.

Your right to object. You can object at any time to processing based on legitimate interests, for reasons relating to your situation, by writing to us. Where Google decides on its own purposes (as with ad measurement), you can also object through Google's consent form.

Personalised ads involve profiling by Google and its partners to choose ads that may interest you. It has no legal or similarly significant effect on you. Bendout makes no automated decisions about you.

8. Children

Bendout is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Children should play with a parent's or guardian's permission, and parents can block purchases with Apple's Screen Time and Ask to Buy. If you think a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.
