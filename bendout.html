Privacy Policy - Bendout

Last Updated: November 23, 2026

This policy explains what Bendout (the "App"), an arrow puzzle game for iPhone and Android published by TuranliApp, does with your information. Where the two versions differ, this policy says so. It covers Bendout only; where the general TuranliApp Privacy Policy differs, this page applies.

In short: no account, no request to track you, no analytics service of our own, and we do not sell your data. Your progress stays on your device and in your own iCloud account (iPhone) or Google account backup (Android). Bendout is free and shows ads from Google AdMob; in the EU, the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google first asks for your consent. Purchases are paid through Apple or Google Play and confirmed by RevenueCat.

3. What stays on your device

Your progress, gold, lives and boosters, levels in progress (including Legend levels in progress), your sound and vibration settings and arrow style, whether the zoom hint was shown, a record of purchases already delivered, the counters the App uses to space out ads, offers and rating requests, Google's record of your consent choices, and RevenueCat's App User ID and purchase status are stored on your device. On iPhone, only levels cleared, stars, best times, gold, lives and boosters with their refill timing, the daily streak and monthly calendar, finished Legend levels and the arrow style are also copied to iCloud (section 2). On Android, all of these except levels in progress, the record of delivered purchases, Google's consent record and RevenueCat's purchase status go to your Google account backup (RevenueCat's App User ID goes to Block Store after a purchase); levels in progress also move with you when you transfer your data to a new phone (section 2). On iPhone, a protected copy of your gold, lives and boosters is also kept in the device's Keychain, which never leaves the device but which iOS may keep after the App is deleted. On Android, a protected copy of your gold, lives and boosters is also kept on the device; it never leaves the device and is deleted with the App. Deleting the App removes everything else on the device, subject to your device backups (section 2).

4. What Bendout does not do

  • No account, and no request for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, location or health data.
  • On iPhone, no App Tracking Transparency prompt and no Advertising Identifier (IDFA). On Android, the advertising ID is used only by Google's ad software, as described in section 2.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting service of our own. If you allow it in your device settings, Apple or Google shares anonymous crash and performance reports with us.
  • No selling of your personal information for money.
  • No access to your payment-card details: purchases are made and paid through Apple or Google Play.

5. Legal bases (GDPR and similar laws)

  • Contract: providing the game, the iCloud or Android backup and the purchases you make, including restoring them.
  • Consent: storing and reading information on your device for advertising, and personalised ads, in the EU, the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, through Google's consent form. You can withdraw consent at any time under Settings › Privacy › Ad privacy choices; this does not affect processing before you withdrew it.
  • Legitimate interests: funding the free game with limited ads, and showing, counting and capping those ads, preventing ad and purchase fraud, and answering your emails.
  • Legal obligation: where the law requires it.

Your right to object. You can object at any time to processing based on legitimate interests, for reasons relating to your situation, by writing to us. Where Google decides on its own purposes (as with ad measurement), you can also object through Google's consent form.

Personalised ads involve profiling by Google and its partners to choose ads that may interest you. It has no legal or similarly significant effect on you. Bendout makes no automated decisions about you.

Depending on where you live, you can ask to access, correct, delete, restrict or port your data, object to processing, and withdraw consent. Bendout has no account, so we hold no profile of you. Purchase records held for us by RevenueCat can be given to you or deleted on request; tell us roughly when you bought what, or send the Apple receipt, or the Google Play order number (it starts with GPA.), so we can find them. For data Google uses for its own purposes, we will help you reach Google. Write to TuranliApp@gmail.com; we reply within one month. You can also complain to a data-protection authority, such as the Austrian Datenschutzbehörde or the one where you live.

California and other U.S. states. We do not sell personal information for money. Outside the EU, the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google may use the advertising data in section 2 to personalise ads, which some U.S. state laws call "sharing" or "targeted advertising". Where a U.S. state privacy law gives you the right to opt out, the App shows Google's ad privacy message and you can opt out at any time under Settings › Privacy › Ad privacy choices. If that option does not appear for you, write to us (section 1) and we will help you reach Google. We do not use or disclose sensitive personal information, and we will not treat you differently for using your rights.

8. Children

Bendout is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Children should play with a parent's or guardian's permission, and parents can block purchases with Apple's Screen Time and Ask to Buy, or with Google Play's purchase authentication and Family Link. If you think a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.
