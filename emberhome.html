Privacy Policy - EmberHome

Last Updated: September 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how EmberHome (the "App"), a shared home and chores application for iOS published by TuranliApp, collects, uses, stores, and protects your information.

This policy covers EmberHome only. TuranliApp publishes other applications that have different features and different data practices; those are covered by our general Privacy Policy. Where the two differ, this document governs your use of EmberHome.

EmberHome helps the people who live together share chores and a shopping list. We have written this policy to describe precisely what the App does. In short: the App stores your account and your home's shared lists so that everyone in the home sees the same thing, it contains no advertising and no tracking software, and it does not ask for access to your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or health data.

By downloading, installing, or using EmberHome, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

You need an account to use EmberHome. Accounts are provided by Firebase Authentication, operated by Google LLC.

2.2 Your home

The following is stored in Cloud Firestore, operated by Google LLC, in its European multi-region (eur3). It belongs to your home and is visible to every member of that home:

DataWhy we store it
Home nameTo identify the home you share
Members: display name, email address, role (owner, admin, or roommate), points, and the date each person joinedTo show who lives in the home, who may invite others, and each person's progress. Other members of your home can see your email address.

Changes to a home are made only through our server functions, which run in Google Cloud's europe-west1 region (Belgium) and check that the request comes from a member of that home.

2.3 Information that stays on your device

  • Your onboarding choices, such as the goal you picked when you first opened the App.
  • A short-lived record of whether your account has Ember Plus, so the App keeps working when you are offline. The App stops relying on it after three days without a fresh confirmation, and removes it when you sign out.
  • A local copy of your home's data, kept by the Firestore software so the App can be used without a connection.

This information is removed when you delete the App.

2.4 Information we do not collect

  • We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone input, or health data. The App requests none of these permissions.
  • We do not collect payment information. Ember Plus is purchased through Apple, and your payment details are handled by Apple, never by us.
  • We do not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA). The App does not ask for permission to track you, because it does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

3. SHARING A HOME WITH OTHER PEOPLE

A home is shared through an invite code, which owners and admins can create in the App. A code opens the home for seven days and for up to twelve people, and creating a new code cancels the previous one.

Please read this carefully. Anyone who obtains a valid invite code can join the home and will then see everything listed in Section 2.2, including the names and email addresses of the other members. Share codes only with the people you live with.

Access is enforced on our servers: only accounts listed as members of a home can read its data, and only through our server functions can it be changed.

4. EMBER PLUS SUBSCRIPTIONS

Ember Plus is an optional auto-renewable subscription, offered monthly or yearly. The yearly plan may start with a free trial. Purchases, renewals, cancellations, and refunds are handled by Apple under your Apple Account.

To check and restore your subscription, the App uses RevenueCat, Inc. RevenueCat receives your EmberHome account identifier (a random ID created by Firebase, not your name or email address), your purchase and subscription history for EmberHome, and technical details sent with each request: your IP address, the identifier Apple assigns to apps from the same developer (identifierForVendor, which is not the advertising identifier), your device model, iOS version, app version, language preferences, and App Store country. It does not receive your payment details or anything in your home's lists. RevenueCat also uses this history to give us subscription statistics, such as how many people start or cancel a subscription.

5. ANALYTICS AND CRASH REPORTS

EmberHome contains no advertising software. It does contain analytics and crash reporting, provided by Google LLC (Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics), used only to understand which parts of the App are used and to find and fix crashes. These reports are not linked to your account: we do not send your account identifier, name, or email address to either service.

Events the App sends:

  • Onboarding: which step was viewed, which goal you picked, and when onboarding finished.
  • Account: that an account was created, signed in (with the method used: Apple or email), signed out, or deleted.
  • Home: that a home was created or joined, or a member's role was changed, with the role involved.
  • Lists: that a task was created, with its room and priority, and that a shopping item was added. Task titles and item names are never sent.
  • Ember Plus: that the subscription screen was shown, where it was shown, which plan was started, whether a restore found an active subscription, and how the screen was left.

Google Analytics for Firebase also records its standard automatic events, such as the first time the App is opened, sessions, and screen views, together with device model, iOS version, app version, language, and an approximate location at the country or city level derived from your IP address. It assigns its own app-instance identifier, which is reset if you delete and reinstall the App.

Crash reports contain the technical state of the App when it crashed or hit an error (such as the stack trace, device model, iOS version, and app version) and an installation identifier created by Crashlytics.

We do not build advertising profiles, and we do not sell or share your data with data brokers.

8. DATA RETENTION AND DELETION

We keep the information in Sections 2.1 and 2.2 for as long as your account exists.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account from within the App, under Profile > Delete account. You are asked to confirm with your password or with Sign in with Apple. This is a permanent deletion, not a sign-out. It removes:

  • Your Firebase Authentication account, and, if you used Sign in with Apple, the App's access to your Apple sign-in.
  • Your user record and your membership of your home.
  • If you are the last member of your home: the home itself, with all of its tasks, shopping list, and invite codes.

If other people still live in the home, the home stays with them. Tasks assigned to you become unassigned, and if you owned the home, ownership passes to another member. Tasks and shopping items you added remain part of the shared home. Invite codes you created for that home keep an internal reference to an account that no longer exists.

Deleting your account does not cancel an Ember Plus subscription. Cancel it in your Apple Account's subscription settings to stop future charges.

Deleted data cannot be recovered. Backup copies held by our infrastructure provider are overwritten in the ordinary course of their retention cycle.

Analytics, crash reports, and subscription records

Analytics and crash reports are retained by Google LLC according to the retention settings of our Firebase project. Deleting your account does not delete them, because they are not linked to your account.

Subscription records are retained by RevenueCat, Inc. according to its own retention policy and are linked to your account identifier. If you would like them deleted after you delete your account, contact us and we will ask RevenueCat to delete them.

9. SECURITY

  • All communication between the App and our infrastructure is encrypted in transit using TLS.
  • Data stored in Cloud Firestore is encrypted at rest by the infrastructure provider.
  • Server-side rules allow only the members of a home to read its data, and only our server functions can change it.
  • Deleting an account requires confirming your identity again at that moment.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
